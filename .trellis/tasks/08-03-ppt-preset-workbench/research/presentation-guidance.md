# Presentation guidance used by the preset design

## Sources

- Microsoft presentation templates: https://create.microsoft.com/en-us/templates/presentations
- Microsoft effective presentation guidance: https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/PowerPoint/tips-for-creating-and-delivering-an-effective-presentation
- Sequoia business plan structure: https://sequoiacap.com/article/writing-a-business-plan/
- Y Combinator pitch deck guidance: https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4T-how-to-design-a-better-pitch-deck

## Applied findings

- Keep slide copy short, readable, high-contrast, and visually consistent.
- Use category-specific narrative structures rather than one generic outline.
- Treat data, timeline, comparison, quotation, cover, and summary pages as semantic layouts, not decoration.
- Pitch decks should move from problem and solution through market, traction, business model, competition, team, and ask.
- Project, annual review, education, analysis, and research presentations need different defaults for density, duration, and page sequence.
